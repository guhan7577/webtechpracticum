Welcome to NovaMotion Robotics

NovaMotion Robotics designs and manufactures industrial robots that help factories, warehouses and hospitals work faster, safer and more accurately. Our robots are used across automotive, electronics, logistics and healthcare industries.

Robot Categories

Industrial Robotic Arms

Multi-axis arms for welding, painting, assembly and material handling.

  • Payload: 5 – 500 kg
  • Reach: up to 3.2 m

Collaborative Robots (Cobots)

Safe, easy-to-program robots that work side by side with people.

  • Payload: 3 – 20 kg
  • Force-limited safety sensors

Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMR)

Self-navigating robots for transport and warehouse logistics.

  • LiDAR-based navigation
